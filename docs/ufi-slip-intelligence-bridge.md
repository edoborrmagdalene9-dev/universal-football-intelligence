# UFI slip-to-intelligence bridge

## What this build does

The browser app already uses Tesseract.js to extract text from one or more uploaded slip screenshots. This build adds a structured review pass over that text:

- identifies likely decimal odds tokens (1.01–1000);
- removes the extracted price from the displayed selection label;
- classifies common market wording (1X2, goals total, both teams to score, double chance, corners, cards, handicap, player and period markets);
- flags ambiguous rows, including lines with multiple decimal values;
- keeps raw OCR text and review notes alongside the parsed fields;
- exposes a CSV export for manual review and later fixture/market matching;
- avoids calculating combined odds when any saved selection has no valid price.

## Use

1. Open the UFI app and go to **Slip & odds lab**.
2. Upload one or more clear, full screenshots and select **Read text from photos (OCR)**.
3. Inspect the extracted text. Fix team names, market labels and odds in the text area if needed.
4. Select **Analyze slip** and inspect each parsed row and its confidence/review note.
5. Export **review CSV** if you want to inspect or process the structured rows outside the browser.
6. Remove bad rows or manually add corrected selections before saving the analysis.

## Important boundaries

- OCR confidence is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability that the row is correct.
- The market classifier recognizes wording only. It does not confirm the bookmaker's internal market ID, fixture identity, settlement rules or selection validity.
- Odds extraction looks for decimal-form odds. Integer-only prices are deliberately not guessed because integers can be scorelines, dates or market thresholds.
- The review CSV is not yet an input to the historical model/market audit. A later fixture-matching stage must resolve teams, competition, kickoff, market type and selection outcome; odds timestamps and permitted source provenance are also required before any historical market evaluation.
- Do not treat the bookmaker's implied probability as an independent model estimate. No edge or betting recommendation is generated by this OCR layer.
- Tesseract.js currently loads from a CDN, so OCR itself needs internet access. Manual text parsing and the rest of the static app can run after the app is loaded, but offline OCR requires bundling the library and language data locally.

## Export schema

`selection, market_guess, decimal_odds, implied_probability, independent_estimate, parse_confidence, correlation_group, warning, raw_line, source`

Empty or uncertain values should remain empty and be verified, not silently imputed.


## Fixture-candidate matching build

The app can now compare explicit “Team A vs Team B” text against the currently loaded StatsBomb competition-season match list. It normalizes common punctuation and club suffixes, matches the team pair in either orientation, and records a candidate match ID/date and home/away orientation only when one exact normalized pair is found. It distinguishes missing opponent, no loaded fixtures, no match in the selected list and ambiguous pairs. Slip rows with recognizable market wording or an explicit fixture can remain in the review even when OCR did not find a decimal price.

### Workflow

1. Load the competition, season and match list in the real-data section.
2. Analyze the slip text.
3. Select **Match selections to loaded fixtures**.
4. Review every candidate and export the updated review CSV.

A candidate is not an authoritative match. Verify competition, date, kickoff, team identity and bookmaker market/settlement rules manually. Single-team selections such as “Arsenal win” are intentionally not assigned an opponent. The current browser matcher does not guess unmentioned fixtures, use fuzzy nearest-name matches, or connect a market to model probabilities yet. This is a conservative first fixture-resolution layer.


## Scenario-to-market bridge build

A new optional **Compare supported markets with current scenario** action compares clearly recognized slip selections against the currently calculated independent-Poisson score grid. It requires a unique fixture candidate and exact agreement between the candidate teams and scenario teams, plus decimal odds. Supported examples are BTTS yes, over/under 1.5/2.5/3.5 goals, explicit home/draw/away result, and common double-chance wording. Unsupported or ambiguous selections remain unestimated rather than being guessed. It displays model probability, raw odds-implied probability, and estimated expected value (p \times odds - 1) for research review.

This is not a recommendation engine: the current scenario is a simplified baseline, not necessarily calibrated for the fixture. The exact market/settlement rule and candidate fixture still require human verification. EV estimates must not be treated as a proven edge without chronological out-of-sample validation, uncertainty checks, and correlation-aware analysis.


## Strict Slip Auditor
The consolidated gate checks OCR confidence, fixture resolution, supported market estimate, positive estimated EV, six current-context checks, source URL, written caveats, loaded lineup evidence and at least 30 locally saved settled forecasts. These are conservative operational gates, not validated universal thresholds. Current-news checks are user-attested; search links do not mean UFI fetched the page. Mark replacement candidates by row number and model them individually. Unqualified slots remain empty.
